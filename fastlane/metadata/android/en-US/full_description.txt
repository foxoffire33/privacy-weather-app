Weather Log is an open-source weather app built on Open-Meteo. It keeps a full history of every city you follow, stored only on your device.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>Never asks for your location: you search for and add cities yourself</li>
<li>No account, no ads, no trackers</li>
<li>All data (cities, history, settings) stays in on-device storage</li>
<li>Optional custom forecast server URL for self-hosted Open-Meteo</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Current conditions, hourly and daily forecasts, and air quality</li>
<li>A daily log of past weather for each city you follow, with historical archive data</li>
<li>Interactive hourly charts</li>
<li>Backup and restore of your cities, history and settings</li>
</ul>
