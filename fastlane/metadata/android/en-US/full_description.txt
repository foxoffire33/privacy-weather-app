Weather Log is an open-source weather app that uses Open-Meteo, an independent third-party weather API that is itself open source (AGPL-3.0) and can be self-hosted. Open-Meteo is not run by the developer of Weather Log. It keeps a full history of every city you follow, stored only on your device. It also works completely offline: you then log the weather yourself.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>Never asks for your location: you search for and add cities yourself</li>
<li>No account, no ads, no trackers, no Google services</li>
<li>All data (cities, history, settings) stays in on-device storage</li>
<li>Offline mode: the app never connects to the internet and you enter cities and weather by hand</li>
<li>Optional custom forecast server URL for self-hosted Open-Meteo</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Current conditions, rain nowcast, hourly and daily forecasts, air quality, pollen and marine data</li>
<li>A daily log of past weather for each city you follow, filled from the historical archive</li>
<li>Edit any logged day by hand, and restore the downloaded values</li>
<li>Trends: compare a period with the same period in earlier years</li>
<li>Interactive charts that open full screen</li>
<li>Backup and restore of your cities, history and settings</li>
<li>24 languages</li>
</ul>
